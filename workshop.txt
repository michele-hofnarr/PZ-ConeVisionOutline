description=
description=Uses the game's built-in vision system. Works on foot and in vehicles, day and night. Hit-list logic from Aim Outline by Kreb (Workshop ID: 3404684285). Works in singleplayer, multiplayer and split-screen.

description=v1.5 changes:
description=
description=Multiplayer and split-screen support. Outlines are calculated locally on each player's own game, so nothing is sent to the server and other players never see your outlines. In split-screen every player gets their own outlines and their own floor-above indicator on their part of the screen.
description=
description=New Sandbox options page "Cone Vision Outline" for server admins. Turn on "Enforce these settings for all players" and the server's values replace every player's own mod options: outline intensity, legacy outline mode, scale by light level, scale by fog, outlines always on, vehicle outlines always on, outline animals. Outline colour stays each player's choice. Changes an admin makes mid-game reach players within one in-game minute. Has no effect in singleplayer. The Sandbox page is translated into English and Russian.
description=
description=Known limitation (split-screen only): if two players both aim with a melee weapon at the same zombies, both see them green. The game paints its hit list for all local players at once and does not tell mods whose hit list it was.
